## Local storage in your browser

The keyboard navigation keeps the current selection in the URL fragment (`#sel=…`). The fragment
never leaves your browser. Nothing is written to cookies, `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`.

## Contacting me

If you write an email, I process your address and the content of your message to answer you
(Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, or (b) if it is about a contract). Messages are deleted once the matter is
settled and no retention duty applies.

## External links

Links to GitHub and other sites are plain links. Data is only transferred to those providers
when you follow a link; their privacy policies apply from then on.
